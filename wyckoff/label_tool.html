Range Labeller

Mark the ranges on your own chart. The range model learns from the boxes you export.

Load your MT5 CSV to start.
Draw a range: drag a rectangle over it. Select a box: click it. dashed = starter box (not yet checked) solid = yours / accepted. When everything on screen up to the right edge is correct, press Checked up to right edge, then move on. Only the checked part of the history is used for training. Keys: ← → move, N next starter box, A accept, Delete remove, R checked. Your work is kept in this browser; export regularly.
